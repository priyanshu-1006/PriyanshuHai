I turned my portfolio into a computer you can explore.

Meet Priyanshu OS.

I wanted the first interaction to feel like sitting down at a computer and discovering what someone has been building.

So it starts with a 3D retro setup. Scroll towards the monitor, enter the desktop, and open an app.

Inside, you’ll find:

• A Windows XP-inspired desktop with draggable windows, startup sounds and a working taskbar.
• 25 project case studies, including Little Code, Little Terminal and Nexsus, with real product screenshots and engineering decisions.
• “This PC” as an interactive map of my tech stack, with links to the projects where I used each tool.
• A terminal to explore my work and a local Chrome-style app to find my social profiles.
• A guided portfolio assistant built around saved questions and follow-ups, plus light/dark themes and accessibility controls.

Built with React, Three.js, Vite and Tailwind CSS.

I’m a third-year CSE student at MMMUT, Gorakhpur, building developer tools and products people use. I’m open to internships and SDE opportunities.

Explore it: https://priyanshu-hai.vercel.app/
